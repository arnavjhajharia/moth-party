Moth Party

one button each · four moths · one lamp

Hold (tap, mouse or Space) to fly straight · let go to spin and aim.
Grab sparks. When the lamp flickers, get out.

CPU moths:

Press your key (or tap your seat) to join. Empty seats are flown by CPU moths, so you can play solo.
Hold to fly straight · let go to spin and aim.
Grab sparks. Ram a rival to knock their sparks loose. When the lamp flickers, get out: the flare burns half your sparks.
Most sparks after 45 seconds wins the round. First to 3 rounds wins the party.
