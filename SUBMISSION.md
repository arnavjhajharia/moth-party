## Title
Moth Party

## One-liner
One button each, four moths, one lamp: a party game you learn in five seconds.

## Description (paste)
Up to four friends share one keyboard (Q, P, Z, M) or one phone or tablet (each player holds a corner of the screen).
Everyone gets exactly one button. Hold it to fly straight, let go to spin and aim. That's the whole control scheme,
and it's surprisingly hard to fly well.

Moths circle a lamp, grabbing the sparks that drift around it. Ram a rival at speed to knock their sparks loose and
steal them. Every few seconds the lamp flickers, and then it flares: anyone still close loses half their glow. The
flares come faster as the round goes on. The moth carrying the most sparks after 45 seconds takes the round, and the
first to three rounds wins the party.

Empty seats are flown by CPU moths (sleepy, buzzing or feral), so you can play alone or with any number of friends
up to four. Every moth plays its own scale on a harp as it collects sparks, so a good run sounds like a rising melody.
The notes are arranged live in code and played on recorded CC0 samples.

## Tools / AI disclosure (paste honestly)
The code was written with an AI coding assistant (Anthropic Claude), directed by the entrant. The art is drawn in code

audio models were used.
